ABOUT

SAAE

From learning to public value

SAAE connects education, research and entrepreneurship so useful knowledge can become capability, evidence and action.

  1. 01 LEARN

    Programs and courses translate AI concepts into useful capabilities for students, professionals and educators.

  2. 02 RESEARCH

    Specialists and researchers connect disciplines, test ideas and strengthen the knowledge Syria can build on.

  3. 03 BUILD

    Entrepreneurship and institutional partnerships help promising work become projects, services and shared capacity.

Specialist Communities

Each community brings specialists together around distinct questions. A shared method allows knowledge and solutions to move across every community.

  • Data

    Turn information into insight.

    Works onCollection and cleaning, modelling, open public data.

  • Smart Urban

    Design smarter, more responsive cities.

    Works onSpatial survey, mobility, the shape of public space.

  • Healthcare

    Apply AI where care matters.

    Works onDiagnostic support, records, reach into underserved areas.

  • Smart Research

    Move ideas from questions to evidence.

    Works onMethod, peer review, publishing what holds up.

  • Software

    Build useful digital systems.

    Works onTools, platforms, integration with what already runs.

  • Smart Economy

    Turn innovation into opportunity.

    Works onProductivity, markets, access to finance.

  • Trainers

    Equip the people who teach others.

    Works onCurriculum, delivery, certification of trainers.

  • Media

    Make knowledge clear and accessible.

    Works onReporting, public literacy, Arabic language material.
