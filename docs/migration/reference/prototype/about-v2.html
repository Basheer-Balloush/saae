ABOUT THE ASSOCIATION

Knowledge grows when we grow it together.

SAAE is a youth-led community turning artificial intelligence and entrepreneurship into practical capability for people across Syria.

Follow the story

01WHO WE ARE

A community built around possibility.

We spread a practical culture of artificial intelligence and entrepreneurship throughout Syrian society.

Young people, students and founders gain the technical and entrepreneurial skills to innovate and take part in shaping the future—not simply watch it arrive.

Training, workshops, scientific events and applied projects connect modern technology with the spirit of initiative, strengthening people while helping promising ideas grow.

02OUR DIRECTION

One root. Two branches.

VISION

A leading platform for Syrian youth.

We aim to empower young Syrians in AI, modern technology and entrepreneurship—and help build a knowledge society able to compete and innovate.

MISSION

Learning that reaches real life.

We provide high-quality education and training that develops technical and entrepreneurial skills, links academic knowledge to practice and creates lasting positive impact.
